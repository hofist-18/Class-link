HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ClassLink.

How do I create a ClassLink account?

Visit the Sign Up page, select your account type and complete the registration process.

How do I access my university portal?

Visit the ClassLink homepage, search for your university and select it to access its login page.

I cannot log into my account. What should I do?

Check that your email address and password are correct. If the problem continues, contact ClassLink Support.

How do students join a class?

Students can join a class using the class code provided by their lecturer.

How do I report a technical problem?

Use the support form below or contact ClassLink through our social media accounts.
