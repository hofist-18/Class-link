About ClassLink

A better way to manage your classroom.

ClassLink is designed to bring the important parts of online learning into one simple platform.

Lecturers can manage their classes, share materials, create assignments, record attendance and manage marks.

Students can join classes, access learning materials, submit assignments, view marks and keep track of attendance.
