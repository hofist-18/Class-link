Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to ClassLink. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you provide when using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you create and use a ClassLink account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account role, and information you provide while using the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information provided through ClassLink to operate the platform and provide features such as classes, learning materials, assignments, marks, attendance, announcements, and communication between students and lecturers.

3. Student and Lecturer Information

Information displayed within a class may be visible to other authorized members of that class. For example, lecturers may need to see the names of students enrolled in their classes in order to manage learning activities.

4. Messages

Messages sent through ClassLink may be stored so that students and lecturers can communicate through the platform.

5. Learning Materials and Assignments

Files and information uploaded to ClassLink may be stored and made available to the authorized users who need access to them.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

ClassLink may use cookies or browser storage to remember preferences and improve the user experience. Our cookie consent feature allows users to accept or reject optional cookies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed through ClassLink. However, no online service can guarantee complete security of information.

8. Your Choices

You may choose whether to accept or reject optional cookies. You should also keep your account password confidential and avoid sharing your login credentials with others.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ClassLink develops new features or changes how information is handled. Updated versions will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled on ClassLink, please contact the ClassLink team.
